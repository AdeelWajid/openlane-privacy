Privacy policy

Openlane · com.logicmint.openlane · Updated 31 October 2026

Who we are

Openlane is an offline arrow puzzle published by Logicmint. The Android package name is com.logicmint.openlane.

This policy explains what the app stores on your device. Questions: privacy@logicmint.app.

What the app does

You tap arrows and clear a board. The game runs on your phone. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sign you in.

Information stored on your device

Progress stays in local storage on the device: the level you have reached, stars, coins, sound and haptic settings, your daily streak, and an unfinished board so you can continue later.

That information is not sent to Logicmint. Uninstalling the app, or using Reset progress in Settings, deletes it.

Information we do not collect

This version does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or a server account. We do not sell personal information. We do not receive your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

The ad button

Hint and heart offers include a short local preview labeled as an ad. In this version that preview is not a third-party advertisement and does not contact an ad network.

If a later version adds a real ad network, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the Play Console data-safety form will be updated to match.

Permissions

The app may vibrate the phone when you turn haptics on. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files outside the app.

Children

Openlane is a general-audience puzzle. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new text will ship in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
